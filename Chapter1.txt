Chapter 1 — Red Alert
The city of Elysium Heights woke before the sun did—sirens in the distance, air thick with tension, and shadows stretching longer than usual. Though nothing looked wrong, something invisible pressed against the skyline, tightening the air like a fist.
Maya Adebayo felt it instantly.
She stepped off the bus and paused. A low hum vibrated through the street, faint but persistent—like a machine buried beneath the pavement. She glanced around. Nobody else reacted. People hurried along, unaware or unwilling to notice.
Maya adjusted her work badge and headed toward the Innovation Hub, an imposing glass tower that reflected the dull morning sky. Just as she reached the entrance, her phone buzzed sharply.
Unknown Number:
“If you enter that building, everything changes.”
She froze.
Spam? A prank?
But something about the message felt deliberate—too precise.
She pocketed the phone and stepped inside.
The lobby was unusually quiet. No jokes from the security guards. No chatter from interns. Even the elevators rose in eerie silence. By the time she reached the twelfth floor, her heartbeat matched the building’s mechanical pulse.
At her desk, she noticed her computer already awake, a single notification blinking.

MANDATORY STAFF ASSEMBLY — 10:00 AM
Attendance required.

Mandatory assemblies weren’t normal. Emergency assemblies were unheard of.
It was 9:46.
Her phone buzzed again.
Unknown Number:
“You’re almost out of time.”
Maya’s pulse spiked. She typed back quickly:
Who is this?
No response.
A sudden blast of noise tore through the office—the fire alarm, but slower, deeper, wrong. The overhead lights cut out, replaced by pulsing red emergency lamps. Every door on the floor slammed shut with a mechanical hiss.
Lockdown.
Colleagues shouted. Some ran. Others froze. The digital monitors in the hallway flickered, then displayed one name in massive red letters:
MAYA ADEBAYO — PRIORITY TARGET
A cold wave rolled over her.
People turned.
Some stepped away from her.
Others whispered.
Her phone buzzed one more time.
Unknown Number:
“They’ve found you. Don’t trust anyone.”
Then the screen went black.
The red lights flashed faster.
Somewhere behind the locked steel doors, footsteps approached.
