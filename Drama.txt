Chapter 1 — The Day Everything Shifted
The morning started like any other in the bustling city of Elysium Heights. Street vendors argued over prices, taxis honked in frustration, and the usual sea of people hurried through their daily routines. But for Maya Adebayo, today felt different—like the air itself was holding its breath.
Maya stepped off the crowded bus, adjusting the strap of her worn backpack. She worked as a junior assistant at the Innovation Hub, a job she never bragged about but held onto tightly. It paid the bills, barely, and kept her dreams alive—dreams she rarely spoke about.
As she approached the towering glass building, her phone buzzed. A message from an unknown number:
“The truth comes out today. Be ready.”
Maya frowned. Probably a wrong number. She shoved the phone back into her pocket and walked through the metal detectors. But the uneasy feeling followed her up all twelve floors.
